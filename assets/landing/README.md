# Landing artwork

The forest background, Game illustration and Engineering illustration were generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool. They are decorative concept illustrations, not screenshots or scientific results. The Portfolio illustration is María's existing `web/images/Portfolio_02_Landscape.png`.

WebP exports are resized and compressed for this landing page. Original artwork is unchanged. Text, borders and links are HTML/CSS.

- `game.webp`

- `portfolio.webp` (existing artwork)

## Generation prompts

### forest

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide 16:9 website background. Beautiful finished painterly fantasy forest clearing at golden hour, massive warm amber tree trunks framing both edges, deep teal woodland in the distance, mossy boulders and wild flowers along a winding footpath at the bottom. Golden sunlight through foliage at upper left. Rich hand-painted environment concept art with visible brushwork, immersive storybook feeling, natural realistic proportions. Composition: the central 75 percent is quieter distant dark teal forest to place three large website cards in front, upper central area dark enough for ivory headline. No text, no UI, no cards, no borders, no watermark, no people. Warm gold and dark forest green, high quality detailed woodland edges.

### game

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape 3:2 illustration for a pixel-art adventure website card. Finished 16-bit pixel art enchanted forest clearing, warm shafts of golden light in dense teal green trees, moss, small flowers and stone path. A small charming witch adventurer in a red brimmed hat and green cloak stands left of center, two tiny violet and pink slimes on the right, an ornate golden treasure chest and a dark stone magical arch with turquoise rune light at the far right. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters, limited palette, professional cozy indie game scene. Fill entire image with artwork. No text, no HUD, no letters, no logos, no border, no watermark.

### engineering

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape 3:2 illustration for an engineering portfolio experience card. A detailed small 3U CubeSat with gold foil, metallic frame and deep blue segmented solar panels floating diagonally above the curved blue Earth horizon. Background: deep midnight indigo and violet outer space with tiny restrained stars, fine pale gold orbital arcs. Warm golden rim light from upper left. Refined hand-painted science-fiction concept art, atmospheric and elegant, visually cohesive with a painterly fantasy portfolio while unmistakably engineering and space. Satellite in central 60 percent, entire silhouette visible, no cropping of panels. No text, no HUD, no numbers, no labels, no watermark, no border.
